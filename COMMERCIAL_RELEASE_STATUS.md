## Security and buyer boundaries

- Seller-reference production isolation proof does not certify a buyer environment or every functional business flow.
- A branch-point recovery proof is not full PITR/RPO/RTO certification.
- Tenant route-security coverage and functional API contract acceptance remain separately scoped from complete business-flow acceptance.
- Signed Android/iOS store artifacts, buyer-specific EAS/signing, physical-device acceptance and app-store publication remain buyer-owned.
- Native-speaker translation review remains outstanding; do not claim human linguistic qualification.
- External identity, messaging/carrier/call-center, payment, webhook, storage, KMS/HSM, maps/road-routing and cloud accounts/licenses/credentials/live tests remain environment-specific.
- The final ZIP SHA-256 and byte length are recorded in the external same-run release statement; a ZIP cannot reliably contain its own final digest.
- CI success does not guarantee zero defects or instant setup in every buyer environment.
